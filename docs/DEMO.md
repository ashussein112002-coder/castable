# Demo script — Castable (2 minutes, one screen, one phone)

**Setup before demos:** the Replit deployment open on `/`; one finished LIVE creator report open in a second
tab (generated during rehearsal, so nothing is computed live on stage); Telegram open on the phone.

**0:00 — The pain (15 s).**
"Creators get brand deals by guessing: what to pitch, to whom, and whether their own videos will pass a
brand-safety check they never see. Brands run that check after the contract, with an intern and a scroll."

**0:15 — The creator flow (40 s).**
Type `@handle` → "Am I castable?". Read the steps out loud while Oriane works: reading your last 12 videos,
finding the creators the algorithm puts next to you (same words on camera, same look), scanning who sponsors
them, writing your pitches. Open the report.

**0:55 — The receipts (35 s).**
Point at: the readiness score with its four components; "Fix before you pitch" with the exact sentence and
the timestamp; the brands buying from creators like you — with the peer's sponsored video as proof; the
pitch with a subject line and an opener the creator can copy.

**1:30 — Video intelligence is the core (15 s).**
"Every number links to a video. Transcripts give us what is said, keyframes what is shown, comments what the
audience asks, visual similarity who you really compete with. Without Oriane this product cannot exist."

**1:45 — The other side + the phone (10 s).**
Switch to "For brands": the same engine returns a vetted shortlist with a client dossier. Show Jarvis on the
phone: `/scout @handle` returns the readiness card in Telegram.

**1:55 — Close (5 s).**
"Built today, solo, on Replit and Oriane. Castable: get cast, with receipts."

**Fallbacks:** no Wi-Fi → the cached report tab still renders; key dead → mock mode is labelled MOCK, say so.
Never demo a run you have not already seen finish.
