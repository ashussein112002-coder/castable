How the score works

  • Relevance {{ '%.0f'|format((W.relevance or 0) * 100) }}% · how much recent work matches the brief (transcript, caption and visual-similarity hits)
  • Performance {{ '%.0f'|format((W.performance or 0) * 100) }}% · ER-per-view percentile in the candidate pool (70%) blended with reach (30%)
  • Audience {{ '%.0f'|format((W.audience or 0) * 100) }}% · follower band, language fit and region
  • Safety {{ '%.0f'|format((W.safety or 0) * 100) }}% · 100 minus flag penalties (high −{{ P.high }}, medium −{{ P.medium }}, low −{{ P.low }}; authenticity note −8)
